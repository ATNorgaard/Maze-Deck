# New settings: places a journey might pass through

**Status:** decided 2026-10-11: **all eight, each a setting of its own** (not
stops on one journey), so the deck can carry any kind of high-fantasy
adventure, with the sides the author chose:

| Setting | Sides |
|---|---|
| The King's Road | outside |
| The Mire | outside |
| The High Road | outside, and inside as a general mountain cave |
| The Drowned Coast | outside, inside |
| The Barrow Moor | outside, inside |
| The Ember Hold | outside (a volcanic landscape, no dwarven gate), inside |
| The Underdark | inside |
| The Sunken Jungle | outside, inside |

Their designs are being drawn for the review page (docs/art-directions/,
NEW-SETTINGS-BRIEF.md). The sketches below were the starting point; the
order they are in no longer means anything.

A setting is content, not mechanics (biomes/types.ts): the engine never reads
it. Adding one means:
- its words: a name, a flavour line, the seven cards' names and reworded
  rules, and about forty narration lines, written for its place;
- its palette and which card family lights it;
- the dark's own way of closing in (the edge at threat);
- its sound bed;
- its art: the back, the vista with five subjects, and the world, inside
  and outside where it has both (AJ7).

Each idea below is sketched to fit the rules the art already lives by.

- **A setting lights itself with one card family's colour, and keeps the
  others small** (AJ4). Only three families light places: Clear Path gold,
  Obstacle teal and Wanderer blue. Today gold lights three settings (the
  dungeon, the tower and the desert), teal two (the deep forest and the
  undercity), and blue one (the frozen pass). New settings that lean blue or
  teal keep the set varied.
- **Item violet and Monster red never light a place.** That rules out a
  purple Underdark or a red hell, so those ideas are re-lit below.
- **Each one is a different kind of place** from the six there are: not
  another built dungeon, not another night forest.

## The ideas, in the order a journey might meet them

### 1. The King's Road: the road out of town
- **Outside:** a crossroads at dusk with a milestone, a roadside shrine and a
  gibbet creaking on its chain; wheat fields, a far town's lights, a lantern
  on a post.
- **Inside:** the roadside inn's common room: the hearth, long tables, a
  bard's corner, travellers who stop talking when you come in.
- **Light:** gold (the setting sun, the lanterns, the hearth).
- **Props:** a cart with a broken wheel, crows on the gibbet, a signpost
  pointing four ways.
- **The dark:** torches moving in the trees (highwaymen), crows gathering,
  the gibbet swinging with no wind.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: a washed-out bridge, or a toll chain across the road;
  - Wanderer: a pedlar with too many pots;
  - Monster: a wolf pack, or a headless rider;
  - Item: a purse in the ditch;
  - Clear Path: the road on under the first stars.
- **Sound:** crickets, wind in the wheat, a far church bell; inside, fire and
  murmur.
- **Why it's worth having:** a gentle first crossing for a new party, and the
  only setting about travel itself.

### 2. The Mire: the swamp
- **Outside:** black water and dead trees in fog, a sunken causeway of
  rotting planks, will-o'-wisps, stilt huts with one lantern lit.
- **Inside:** a bog-witch's stilt hut (herbs hanging, bones, a cauldron), or
  a half-flooded shrine.
- **Light:** teal: the marsh-light and the wisps, kept teal-green and well
  away from the Trap card's acid yellow-green.
- **Props:** reeds, lily pads, hanging moss, eyes just above the waterline.
- **The dark:** the water rises toward the table, the wisps drift in to lure,
  hands break the surface.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: the boardwalk sinking into the bog;
  - Wanderer: the witch with her lantern;
  - Monster: a hag, or something long rising from the water;
  - Item: a bottle tree hung with lights;
  - Clear Path: firm ground and a dry path.
- **Sound:** frogs, insects, bubbles rising, a heron's cry.

### 3. The High Road: a mountain pass in a summer storm
- **Outside:** a road cut into a cliff face, a rope bridge over a gorge,
  waterfalls, eagles, a thunderstorm with lightning far off.
- **Inside:** a mountain waystation or cliff monastery: a cold hall, prayer
  flags, a single brazier.
- **Light:** blue (storm light and lightning), with gold where the sun breaks
  through.
- **Props:** goat paths, cairns, a broken rope bridge, wind-bent pines.
- **The dark:** the storm closes in, lightning strikes nearer, rockfall from
  above.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: the rope bridge snapped;
  - Wanderer: a goatherd;
  - Monster: a giant on the ridge, or a roc's shadow;
  - Item: an offering at a shrine;
  - Clear Path: a gap in the cloud.
- **Sound:** wind, thunder, the creak of rope.
- **Overlap:** the frozen pass is already a mountain pass, in snow. This one
  only earns its place as a different season and weather. Folding it into
  the frozen pass is the alternative.

### 4. The Drowned Coast
- **Outside:** sea cliffs in a storm, a wreck broken on the rocks, a
  lighthouse sweeping its beam, gulls.
- **Inside:** a smugglers' sea cave of barrels and lanterns, or the wreck's
  hold, half flooded and listing.
- **Light:** gold (the lighthouse beam, the lanterns) against storm blue.
- **Props:** nets, a figurehead, rock pools, a bell on a buoy.
- **The dark:** the tide comes in, drowned sailors' lights out on the water,
  the bell ringing by itself.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: the tide cutting off the path;
  - Wanderer: a beachcomber;
  - Monster: a merrow or a sea hag;
  - Item: a chest washed up;
  - Clear Path: the beam finding the way.
- **Sound:** surf, gulls, timber groaning.

### 5. The Barrow Moor: the edge of the Shadowfell
- **Outside:** standing stones and burial mounds under a huge pale moon,
  mist in the hollows, a ruined chapel.
- **Inside:** a barrow tomb of grave goods, stone coffins and ghost-light.
- **Light:** blue: a pale spectral blue, on a grey-green moor rather than the
  frozen pass's ice.
- **Props:** crows, a lychgate, a dead tree with ribbons.
- **The dark:** ghost-lights drift in, the mounds stir, whispering at the
  edge.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: a sealed tomb door;
  - Wanderer: the gravedigger;
  - Monster: a wight;
  - Item: grave goods;
  - Clear Path: a path between the stones.
- **Sound:** wind over grass, a far bell, whispers.

### 6. The Ember Hold: a dwarven forge under a volcano
- **Outside:** lava fields under an ash sky, and a road of black glass to a
  gate carved in the mountain.
- **Inside:** the forge hall: molten metal running in channels, giant
  anvils, chains, statues of dwarf-kings.
- **Light:** gold, the molten metal kept gold and orange and never red (red
  is the Monster's).
- **Props:** rails and carts, bellows, rune-carved doors.
- **The dark:** heat haze closing in, embers swarming, the floor cracking to
  glow.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: a lava channel with its bridge fallen;
  - Wanderer: a soot-black smith;
  - Monster: a salamander or a fire elemental;
  - Item: a rune-axe cooling on the anvil;
  - Clear Path: a cooled crust you can cross.
- **Sound:** a deep rumble, hammers, steam hissing.

### 7. The Underdark: miles below (inside only)
- **One side:** a cavern so vast it has weather: fungus forests taller than
  trees, chasms crossed by spider-silk bridges, a lightless lake, a drow city
  far off.
- **Light:** blue: cold bioluminescence from glowing fungus, lichen and
  crystal. The faerzress is violet in the books, but violet is the Item's
  colour, so here it glows blue-white.
- **Props:** webs, crystal stalactites, a rope-and-silk bridge, a boat on the
  black lake.
- **The dark:** spiders descending on threads, clusters of small eyes, the
  fungus light going out patch by patch.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: the bridge cut, or a wall of web;
  - Wanderer: a deep gnome, or an exiled drow;
  - Monster: something with too many legs;
  - Item: a glowing geode;
  - Clear Path: the silk bridge holding.
- **Sound:** a vast low drone, drips into still water, clicking far away.
- **How it differs:** the undercity is a city's sewers and the dungeon is
  built. The Underdark is wild, deep and enormous.

### 8. The Sunken Jungle
- **Outside:** steaming jungle, vines and giant leaves, a step pyramid
  breaking the canopy, a waterfall.
- **Inside:** the temple: water channels, carved serpents, a gold idol on its
  altar.
- **Light:** gold: shafts of sun through the canopy, the greens desaturated
  so they never read as the Obstacle's teal or the Trap's yellow-green.
- **Props:** rope bridges between trees, carved heads swallowed by roots.
- **The dark:** the vines creep in, insect swarms, eyes in the leaves.
- **Cards:**
  - Obstacle: a collapsed stair;
  - Wanderer: a guide;
  - Monster: a great serpent, or a raptor;
  - Item: the idol;
  - Clear Path: sunlight down the stair.
- **Sound:** birds, rain on leaves, water falling.

## Recommendation (superseded: the author took all eight)

Three first, as a journey's arc: **the King's Road** (the road out of town),
**the Mire** (the wild between), and **the Underdark** (the deep). They are
three kinds of place the six don't cover; they light with gold, teal and
blue, one each; and two of them are places the author named.

- **The mountain pass you named:** the frozen pass already is one, so the
  High Road is only worth adding as a storm-and-summer counterpart. Otherwise
  fold it into the frozen pass.
- **Seven or more settings change the threshold:** the doors become a scroll
  rather than a row (front-door phase 3), which that plan already allows.

Each picked setting would go through what the six did: two directions drawn
(inside and outside where it has both) on the review page, then its words,
palette, dark and sound, and only then a build.
