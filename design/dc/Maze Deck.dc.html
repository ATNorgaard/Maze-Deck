The River

THE RIVER

A travel-time sidecar for your campaign. Three paths at a time, twenty-eight cards, five checks your characters already have. Run the dungeon, the tower, the forest — then get back to the table.

One GM · up to six seats · no install
§1

The river

1.1The river is always three cards wide. An empty slot holds its width and refills from the deck at the end of the turn; the row never closes up.

1.2Cards enter face-down. A player commits to a path before knowing what is in it — acting on incomplete information is the whole design.

1.3Dead End and Obstacle persist in their slot until an action clears them. Three blockers at once: clear all three and a Monster enters the river.

1.4Collect five Clear Paths and the party is through. Two Monsters revealed and the journey ends in a confrontation instead — which is where you take over.

§3

Seven paths, twenty-eight possibilities

Every card is the same arch; only what stands in it changes. The glyph and the corner shape identify the category — colour only confirms it. Hover a card to bring it to the front.
§4

Five actions, keyed to the sheet you already have

One action per player per turn, taken before any card is turned. Each is a check against the Maze DC using an ability modifier copied straight off the character sheet. At the end of a round a d6 locks one action for the round to come.
